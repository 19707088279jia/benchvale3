About ChromVale

Focused on HPLC Columns for Canadian Laboratories

ChromVale Scientific is a Canadian supplier based in Waterloo, Ontario. We help laboratories identify suitable HPLC column categories and specifications, then organize the practical information needed for purchasing decisions.

Our focus is deliberately narrow: HPLC columns, available manufacturer documentation, selection assistance, and a clear quotation workflow. Customers can share their analytical requirements so potential product options can be reviewed for further evaluation.

We aim to make the purchasing process easier to navigate for Canadian laboratories, with written support for product questions, documentation requests, delivery details, and eligible post-purchase concerns.
