BENCHVALE HOMEPAGE BANNER — QUICK IMAGE REPLACEMENT

Fastest method:
1. Prepare a wide image close to 1600 x 650 px (JPG, PNG, WebP, or SVG).
2. The four current image files are in the images folder:
   banner-general-lab.svg
   banner-analytical.svg
   banner-liquid-handling.svg
   banner-procurement.svg
3. To swap an image, upload your replacement into GitHub's images folder and update the corresponding <img src="images/..."> line near the top of index.html if the filename or extension changes.
4. Commit the change. GitHub Pages normally updates in 1–3 minutes.

Banner text/buttons:
Edit the four <article class="home-slide"> blocks near the top of index.html.

Auto-rotation timing:
In script.js, find 5500. This is 5.5 seconds. Example: 7000 = 7 seconds.

Recommendation:
Use supplier/product photography only when you have permission to publish it on your website.
