Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This policy explains how ChromVale Scientific handles information you choose to provide through this website and by email.

1

Information You Provide

Information submitted when you contact ChromVale.

When you contact ChromVale Scientific, you may provide information such as your name, organization, business email address, phone number, product requirements, quantities, postal code, required date, specifications, and any other details you choose to include.

2

Contact and Availability Request Forms

How website inquiries are delivered to ChromVale.

Contact and availability request forms are submitted through Formspree, a third-party form-processing service, and delivered to ChromVale Scientific so that we can respond to your inquiry or quotation request.

Information submitted through these forms may therefore be processed by Formspree in accordance with its service and privacy practices. Please do not submit confidential, regulated, or sensitive personal data.

3

How We Use Your Information

Purposes connected with inquiries and potential orders.

We use information you provide to respond to inquiries, evaluate HPLC column requirements, prepare column options and quotations, communicate about potential or approved orders, and provide related customer or technical support.

4

Information Sharing and Retention

Service providers, marketing, and business records.

We do not sell your personal information or share it with third parties for their own marketing purposes. Information may be handled by service providers used for normal website, email, or business operations where necessary to provide those services.

We retain business correspondence and related information for as long as reasonably necessary for inquiry handling, order administration, customer support, and legitimate business record-keeping.

5

Inquiry Forms and Browser Storage

Quote Cart data and information that should not be submitted.

Availability, quotation, and contact submissions are processed by Formspree and delivered to ChromVale for inquiry handling and follow-up. Please do not include confidential, regulated, payment-card, or patient information in these forms.

This website uses browser local storage to retain items added to the Quote Cart. You can clear this information through your browser settings or by clearing the Quote Cart. Online checkout is currently disabled, and payment-card details are not collected or stored by this website.

6

Contact

Questions about this privacy policy.

Questions about this policy can be directed to eric@chromvale.com.
