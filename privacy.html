Privacy Notice

How we handle information you provide

This notice explains how ChromVale Scientific handles information you choose to provide through this website and by email.

Last updated: August 2026

01

Information you provide

When you contact ChromVale Scientific, you may provide information such as your name, organization, business email address, phone number, product requirements, quantities, postal code, required date, specifications, and any other details you choose to include.

02

Contact and Request a Quote forms

Contact and Request a Quote forms are submitted through Formspree, a third-party form-processing service, and delivered to ChromVale Scientific so that we can respond to your inquiry or quotation request.

Information submitted through these forms may therefore be processed by Formspree in accordance with its service and privacy practices. Please do not submit confidential, regulated, or sensitive personal data.

03

How we use your information

We use information you provide to respond to inquiries, evaluate equipment requirements, prepare product or equipment options and quotations, communicate about potential or approved orders, and provide related customer or technical support.

04

Information sharing and retention

We do not sell your personal information or share it with third parties for their own marketing purposes. Information may be handled by service providers used for normal website, email, or business operations where necessary to provide those services.

We retain business correspondence and related information for as long as reasonably necessary for inquiry handling, order administration, customer support, and legitimate business record-keeping.

05

Shopify, checkout, and browser storage

Direct-order product, cart, customer-account, payment, tax, delivery, and checkout services are provided through Shopify. Information entered during Shopify Checkout is handled under Shopify's applicable privacy and security practices as well as ChromVale's order-administration requirements.

This website uses browser local storage to retain the Shopify cart identifier and the items added to the Quote Cart. You can clear this information through your browser settings or by clearing the applicable cart. Payment-card details are not collected or stored by this website.

06

Contact

Questions about this notice can be directed to eric@chromvale.com.
