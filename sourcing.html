Sourcing Support

Support for products beyond the catalogue

If you have a preferred manufacturer, Cat.No., application, or technical specification, Benchvale can review potentially suitable product or equipment options and confirm the details before an order proceeds.

Request a Quote

Four Steps

Simple, reviewable, and requirement-driven

The goal is to narrow the request into equipment options your team can review before an order is placed.

01

Tell us what you need

Share the equipment type, application, key specifications, quantity, budget, and timeline you already know.

02

We identify suitable options

We review the requirements and identify potentially suitable equipment options based on the request.

03

Review specifications and pricing

Your team reviews the relevant specifications and pricing information for the proposed options.

04

Confirm before ordering

No product or equipment is ordered until you have reviewed the proposed option and approved the order in writing.

Quotation Review

Commercial and technical details in one place

Public product pages use “Request a Quote.” The applicable product configuration, pricing, availability, shipping assumptions, documentation, and product-specific terms are presented for review before an order.
