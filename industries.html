Industries We Serve

Laboratories we can assist

Our primary focus is environmental, water and wastewater testing, and analytical laboratories, with sourcing tailored to technical, operational, budget, and timeline needs.

Environmental Laboratories

Equipment sourcing support for environmental sample preparation, filtration, handling, and routine analysis.

Typical sourcing needs
  • Mixers & shakers
  • Vacuum & filtration equipment
  • Sample preparation equipment

Water & Wastewater Testing Laboratories

Sourcing support for equipment used in water and wastewater sample handling, filtration, transfer, and routine testing workflows.

Typical sourcing needs
  • Peristaltic & dosing pumps
  • Vacuum & filtration equipment
  • Benchtop testing instruments

Analytical Laboratories

Sourcing support for analytical preparation, sample handling, and dependable routine bench workflows.

Typical sourcing needs
  • Mixers, shakers & stirrers
  • Sample preparation equipment
  • Benchtop support equipment
Other laboratory environments

We also review requests from academic and research laboratories and industrial or quality-control laboratories when the equipment and support requirements are practical to manage.
